EcoFlow River 2 Max

A 512Wh power station capable of 500W output. Perfect for camping and outdoors.

Current Best Deal at EcoFlow Official

If you are looking for reliable off-grid energy, the EcoFlow River 2 Max is a contender worth considering. The unit is built around a 512Wh battery pack. You get a steady 500W of output, which covers everything from laptops to small appliances. Perfect for keeping drones, cameras, and laptops charged.

It is relatively easy to move around at 6.1kg. It is well-equipped with 4 AC sockets for your appliances. Overall, this is a solid package for the price.
